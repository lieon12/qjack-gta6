# Solmar Bay — Open World Crime

Solmar Bay is a complete top-down open-world crime game set in an original sun-soaked coastal city: explore on foot or steal and drive cars through traffic and pedestrians, raise a five-star wanted level with police cars and foot cops who chase and arrest you, and run five story missions for Mama Rosa — from your first car theft to stopping an armored van and escaping with the cash — with combat, a weapon and armor shop, cash pickups, a hospital, a minimap, a HUD, save/continue, and synthesized sound and music, all in plain static files with no network request.
