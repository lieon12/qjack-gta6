# Solmar Bay — Open World Crime

Solmar Bay is a complete top-down open-world crime game set in an original sun-soaked coastal city: explore on foot or steal and drive cars through traffic and pedestrians, raise a five-star wanted level with police cars and foot cops who chase and arrest you, and run eight story missions for Mama Rosa — from your first car theft to stopping an armored van and escaping with the cash, plus the three-mission "Tiburon Rising" arc (Casino Lights, Marina Tide, The Shark Surfaces) — with combat, a weapon and armor shop, cash pickups, a hospital, a minimap, a HUD, save/continue, and synthesized sound and music, all in plain static files with no network request.

Revision: a day/night cycle (a 24-hour clock, night darkness and a moon glow; N skips two hours) and a weather system (Clear, Cloudy, Rain and Fog — rain cuts driving grip and visibility); six distinct vehicle models to steal and drive (Comet GT, Solmar Sedan, Bay Van, Tiburon Limo, Marina Speedster and City Taxi, each with its own speed, acceleration, handling and silhouette); a police helicopter that spawns at three wanted stars, hovers with a spotlight and holds the heat up until the player loses it; taxi fares (press T for duty, pick up the flagged passenger, drive them to the destination before the timer runs out and earn the fare); and three new districts on the map and minimap — the neon Strip, the Casino row with its gold towers, and the Marina with piers and moored boats.
